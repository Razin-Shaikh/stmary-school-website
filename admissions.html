Admissions

Join St. Mary's School

Newasa

We welcome students who are eager to learn, grow and achieve excellence.

Admission Process

Admissions are open for eligible students subject to seat availability and the school's admission policy.

Parents are requested to visit the school office for complete information regarding admissions and fee structure.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Steps to Apply

Step 1

Collect or download the admission form.

Step 2

Submit the completed form with documents.

Step 3

Complete verification and confirm admission.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

Documents for Admission

Please carry original documents along with self-attested photocopies during admission.

Birth Certificate

Original birth certificate issued by the competent authority.

Previous School Record

Report card, Leaving Certificate (LC) and Bonafide Certificate (if applicable).

Passport Photos

Recent passport-size photographs of the student and parents.

Aadhaar Card

Copy of the student's Aadhaar Card (if available).

Address Proof

Aadhaar Card, Electricity Bill or any valid address proof.

Medical Record

Health details or vaccination record if required by the school.

Eligibility

Admissions are granted based on the age criteria prescribed for each class and subject to seat availability.

Students seeking admission to higher classes should submit previous academic records and required certificates.

The school reserves the right to conduct an interaction or assessment wherever applicable.
