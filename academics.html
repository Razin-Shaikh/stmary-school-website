Academic Overview

St. Mary's School follows a structured curriculum designed to provide a strong academic foundation while encouraging critical thinking, creativity and moral development.

Our teaching methodology combines classroom instruction, practical activities, projects, technology-enabled learning and continuous assessment.

CURRICULUM

Academic Curriculum

Primary

Foundational education focusing on literacy, numeracy and character building.

Middle School

Strengthening concepts through activity-based learning and practical experiences.

Secondary

Preparing students for board examinations and higher education.
