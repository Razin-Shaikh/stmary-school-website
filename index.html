About Our School

St. Mary's School, Newasa is an English Medium Convent School managed by the Ursuline Sisters of Mary Immaculate.

Our mission is to educate young minds through academic excellence, moral values, discipline, compassion and holistic development.

We believe every child deserves opportunities to discover their talents and become responsible, confident citizens.

From Our Leadership

Principal's Message

Sr. Jyothis Joseph UMI

Welcome to St. Mary's School – Where Every Child Learns, Grows, and Shines. It is with immense joy and gratitude that I welcome you to our institution.

Welcome to St. Mary's School – Where Every Child Learns, Grows, and Shines. Dear Parents, Students, and Visitors,

It is with immense joy and gratitude that I welcome you to St. Mary's School. Thank you for taking the time to learn more about our institution. Choosing the right school for your child is one of the most important decisions you will make, and we are honoured that you are considering to become a part of our St. Mary's family.

Academic excellence remains one of our highest priorities. Our dedicated and passionate teachers work tirelessly to provide meaningful learning experiences that prepare students for the challenges of an ever-changing world. At the same time, we recognize that true education extends beyond textbooks. Through sports, cultural activities, leadership opportunities, social outreach programmes, and various co-curricular activities, we encourage our students to become confident individuals, compassionate leaders, and responsible citizens.

At St. Mary's School, we also place great importance on values such as honesty, discipline, respect, kindness, responsibility, and service. Inspired by the teachings of Christ and guided by Gospel values, we strive to form young people who not only excel in their careers but also make a positive difference in society through their integrity and compassion. We firmly believe that education is a shared responsibility between the school and the family. Together, through mutual trust, open communication, and shared commitment, we can provide every child with the guidance, encouragement, and support needed to flourish academically, emotionally, socially, and spiritually.

I warmly invite you to visit our campus, interact with our faculty, and experience the welcoming atmosphere that makes St. Mary's School a vibrant community of learning. I am confident that you will find a place where children are inspired to learn, encouraged to lead, and prepared to serve. Let us work together to nurture young minds, strengthen values, and build a future filled with knowledge, faith, compassion, and hope.

"Education is not only about making a living; it is about making a life."

I look forward to welcoming you and your family to St. Mary's School, where we strive to educate minds, inspire hearts, and transform lives. With warm regards and best wishes, Sr. Jyothis Joseph UMI, Principal, St. Mary's School

Manager's View

Sr. Maria Carvalho

At St. Mary's School, Newasa, we believe that true education goes beyond academic excellence. It is about nurturing young minds.

At St. Mary's School, Newasa, we believe that true education goes beyond academic excellence. It is about nurturing young minds with knowledge, compassion, integrity, discipline and a strong sense of responsibility.

Our endeavour is to provide every student with an education rooted in values and ethics, enabling them to distinguish right from wrong, respect others, appreciate diversity and grow into confident, caring and responsible citizens.

We envision a future where our students carry the values they learn today into the world of tomorrow—using their knowledge and talents to serve humanity and contribute positively to society.

May every child who walks through the doors of St. Mary's School discover the joy of learning, the strength of character and the courage to make a difference.

ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE

22 Years of 100% SSC Results

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

St. Mary's School, Newasa has proudly maintained a 100% pass percentage in the SSC Board Examination for the past 22 consecutive years. This outstanding achievement reflects the dedication of our students, the commitment of our experienced teachers, and the continuous support of parents.

Every academic year, our students have consistently secured excellent results, with many achieving distinctions and scoring above 90%, bringing laurels to the school.
